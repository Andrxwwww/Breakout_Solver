Breakout AI

A small neural network that taught itself to play Breakout. Nobody showed it the right moves: a genetic algorithm just kept the networks that scored best, game after game. Written from scratch in Java, no libraries.

How it works

The network sees where the ball, the paddle and the brick are, and where the ball will land. It answers with one move: stay, left or right.

To train it, 100 networks play random games. The best ones are kept, mixed and slightly changed, and this repeats 400 times.

The trick it learned is aiming: where the ball hits the paddle decides where it flies next, so it moves to send the ball into the brick.
